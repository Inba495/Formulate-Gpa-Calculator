Quick Guide to the Formulas:
For flat 2D shapes like a rectangle, area is just length × width, and perimeter is adding up all four sides (2l + 2w). For a circle, area is πr² and the distance around it (circumference) is 2πr.

For 3D stuff, a sphere's volume uses (4/3)πr³. A cylinder calculates volume using the base circle area times height (πr²h), plus surface area by adding the top/bottom caps to the side wall. A torus uses 2π²Rr². This is created by Inba Jeyakumar
